The problem: handovers run on paper & voice notes

In Colombia and across Latin America, industrial shift handovers still run on paper notes and WhatsApp voice messages.

Most facilities have no digital system to capture what happened during a shift — unlike plants in the US or Europe.

An open alarm nobody logged.
A leaking valve nobody flagged.
A “keep an eye on this” that never survived the conversation.
Supervisors find out days late.

The hard part

Grounded disambiguation

Operator says “the line 3 pump” — two real assets match: P-301A and P-301B, both centrifugal transfer pumps on line 3, both critical.

The agent does NOT guess. It stops and asks.
Catalog is the only answer set. The plant's real asset catalog — nothing invented.
Aliases & parent assets. Colloquial names resolve as reliably as formal tags.
